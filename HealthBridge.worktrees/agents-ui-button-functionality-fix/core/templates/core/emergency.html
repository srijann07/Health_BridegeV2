⚠️ Important Emergency Information

  • Call emergency services immediately for severe emergencies
  • Provide accurate location information for faster response
  • Keep your emergency contacts updated
  • Inform the responder about any allergies or medical conditions
  • Follow first aid instructions given by paramedics
  • Keep medical documents accessible
